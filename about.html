About me

Who I am, how I work and the tools I use.

Hi, I'm Lorenzo

A Computer Engineering student at Politecnico di Torino who doesn't like staying in one lane. You'll find me diving into anything from AI algorithms to practical applications, and game development is where all of it meets.

Outside of code I'm a chess enthusiast and a logic puzzle lover. Both have shaped how I work: determined, patient and always trying to think three moves ahead.

I'm a team player at heart and do my best work with people who share the same drive, like the Level Up Lab crew I build games with. I hold a Cambridge C2 Proficiency (CPE) certificate, so working in English is never a problem.
